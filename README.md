PETIR — Guardian of the Monsoon: fly a storm kite over monsoon rice paddies, blast shadow spirits with lightning, survive 10 waves and defeat the Storm King. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best game” bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

## How to play

- **WASD / arrow keys** — fly the kite
- **Mouse** — aim
- **Click / Space** — fire lightning
- **P** — pause, **M** — sound, **R** — restart

Survive all 10 waves. The Storm King waits at the end of wave 10 — bring him down and dawn breaks. Pick up power-ups: Spread Shot, Rapid Fire, Shield, Extra Life. Chain kills to raise your combo multiplier up to ×8.
